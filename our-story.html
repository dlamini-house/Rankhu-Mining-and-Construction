Our Story

Our Story

RANKHU Mining Pty Ltd was established in 2015 with a clear vision: to become a trusted and reliable supplier within the chrome ore industry by consistently delivering quality, integrity, and performance.

The company was founded by Makgati Rangoato, whose entry into the industry began with a single opportunity to supply his first batch of chrome ore. This initial engagement was with a highly demanding client, setting rigorous standards from the outset. Rather than viewing this as a challenge to overcome, Makgati embraced it as a defining moment. His commitment to meeting and exceeding expectations laid the foundation for the company's operational discipline, attention to detail, and customer-centric approach.

That first successful delivery marked the beginning of a long-term relationship built on trust and consistency — principles that continue to guide RANKHU Mining today.

Since its inception, the company has experienced steady and sustainable growth. By reinvesting in operations and expanding its workforce, RANKHU Mining has developed the capacity to meet increasing market demands while contributing to job creation and skills development. Our team remains a cornerstone of our success, enabling us to maintain high standards across all aspects of our operations.

RANKHU Mining has established a strong reputation for the supply of high-quality chrome ore. Through a disciplined focus on product quality, logistical efficiency, and dependable service delivery, the company has successfully expanded its client base from local markets to international customers. Today, we are proud to participate in global supply chains, representing South African mining excellence on an international platform.

Our approach is defined by long-term partnerships rather than short-term transactions. We work closely with our clients to understand their specific requirements and ensure consistent supply that meets industry specifications and timelines. This collaborative mindset has been instrumental in building lasting relationships across the value chain.

Looking ahead, RANKHU Mining is strategically positioned for further growth and diversification. In addition to strengthening our presence in the chrome ore sector, the company is actively exploring opportunities within the gold market. This expansion reflects our commitment to evolving with industry demands while maintaining the same standards of quality and reliability that have defined our journey.

At RANKHU Mining Pty Ltd, we remain driven by the principles that shaped our beginnings — resilience, accountability, and a commitment to excellence. As we continue to grow, our focus remains on delivering value to our clients, empowering our people, and contributing meaningfully to the mining sector.

Forward Looking

The Vision

Precision

At RANKHU Mining Pty Ltd, precision defines every aspect of our operations. From resource selection and grade control to logistics and delivery, we operate with a disciplined focus on accuracy and consistency. Our processes are designed to ensure that every tonne of material meets strict specification standards, supported by structured sampling and verified laboratory analysis. Precision is not just a technical requirement — it is a core operating principle that underpins our reputation in the global mineral market.

Strength

Our strength lies in our operational resilience, technical capability, and ability to deliver consistently in demanding market conditions. RANKHU Mining has built a robust foundation within the chrome industry through reliable supply performance, scalable operations, and strong supply chain integration. This strength is reinforced by our people, our partnerships, and our commitment to maintaining stability and reliability across all projects and market cycles.

Commitment

Commitment is central to our identity. We are committed to delivering high-quality mineral products, maintaining ethical and responsible mining practices, and building long-term partnerships with our clients and stakeholders. Our commitment extends beyond supply — it includes consistency, accountability, and continuous improvement in everything we do. We remain focused on sustainable growth while contributing meaningfully to the mining industry and the broader economy.
