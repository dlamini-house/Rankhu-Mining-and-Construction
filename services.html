Our Services

Integrated solutions across chrome mining, gold development, and construction support. Delivered with operational excellence and technical precision.

We deliver engineered solutions for complex mining and construction environments, driven by disciplined execution and commitment to operational excellence.

01

Chrome

RANKHU Mining Pty Ltd is a producer and supplier of high-grade chrome ore, positioned within the global mineral supply chain. Our operations are built on disciplined resource sourcing, stringent grade control, and consistent product delivery aligned with international market specifications.

Quality assurance is central to our chrome business. All material is subjected to systematic sampling protocols and independently verified through reputable, certified analytical laboratories. This ensures accurate grade confirmation, transparency in reporting, and full compliance with client and export requirements.

Our integrated operational model covers beneficiation oversight, stockpile management, and logistics coordination, enabling us to maintain product integrity from source to final delivery. This structured approach allows RANKHU Mining to reliably serve both domestic and international markets with consistent, specification-driven chrome ore supply.

02

Gold

RANKHU Mining is actively positioning itself for strategic participation within the gold sector through targeted exploration, project evaluation, and partnership-driven development opportunities.

Our approach is grounded in technical due diligence, resource validation, and long-term value creation. We are focused on identifying viable gold assets and structuring scalable projects that meet both operational and investment-grade standards.

The company is open to strategic joint ventures and capital partnerships aimed at accelerating responsible gold development and unlocking sustainable mineral value.

03

Construction

RANKHU Mining provides integrated construction and mining support capabilities that enhance operational efficiency across project lifecycles. Our services include site preparation, bulk earthworks, infrastructure development, and environmental rehabilitation.

These capabilities are supported by coordinated project execution, plant and equipment utilization, and structured site logistics management. Our objective is to ensure that mining and construction projects are executed with precision, safety, and operational continuity.

We also place strong emphasis on environmental stewardship, ensuring that all post-mining rehabilitation activities align with regulatory standards and sustainable land restoration principles.

Integrated Execution

Operational Matrix

A comprehensive overview of our technical competencies and structured operational approach across all project phases.

Site Clearance

At RANKHU Mining Pty Ltd, site clearance is executed with a structured and safety-driven approach to ensure that all mining areas are properly prepared for operational activity. Each site undergoes a preliminary assessment to evaluate surface conditions, vegetation density, soil structure, and any environmental or regulatory considerations.

Our clearing process includes vegetation removal, debris management, and topsoil stripping where required. Topsoil is carefully preserved and stockpiled for future rehabilitation, supporting responsible land management practices. All activities are conducted in line with environmental compliance standards and industry best practices.

We utilize appropriate machinery and trained personnel to ensure efficient execution while maintaining strict safety protocols. This ensures that sites are prepared in a controlled, compliant, and environmentally responsible manner.

Excavation

Excavation is a core operational function at RANKHU Mining, carried out with precision, discipline, and technical oversight. Our approach is guided by site-specific planning and an understanding of geological conditions to ensure accurate and efficient extraction.

We deploy suitable excavation equipment based on ground conditions and material characteristics, enabling controlled removal of overburden and chrome ore. Grade control is strictly maintained throughout the process to reduce dilution and ensure product consistency.

Safety is central to all excavation activities. We implement continuous monitoring, operational controls, and adherence to mining regulations to maintain a safe working environment. Environmental considerations are also integrated into our excavation methodology, including responsible waste handling and support for rehabilitation processes where applicable.

Plant Operations

Plant operations at RANKHU Mining Pty Ltd are managed with a strong focus on efficiency, reliability, and product quality. Our processing systems are designed to ensure optimal handling, screening, and preparation of chrome ore to meet client specifications.

We operate and maintain plant equipment through structured maintenance schedules, performance monitoring, and operational controls that minimize downtime and maximize productivity. Our team ensures that all machinery is operated within safe and efficient parameters, supporting consistent output quality.

Quality assurance is embedded within our plant operations, with regular sampling and inspection processes conducted to ensure compliance with required grade specifications. This disciplined approach allows us to consistently deliver a reliable and market-ready product.

Through integrated plant management and technical oversight, RANKHU Mining maintains operational excellence across all processing activities.
