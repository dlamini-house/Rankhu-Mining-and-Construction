Who Rankhu Is

RANKHU Mining Pty Ltd is a South African mining and mineral resources company established in 2015. The business has developed from a single trading opportunity into a structured mining operator supplying high-grade chrome ore to domestic and international markets.

Our operations are built on disciplined execution, technical oversight, and consistent delivery of specification-driven mineral products supported by verified quality assurance and reliable logistics.

What We Do

We operate across three core areas: chrome ore production and supply, emerging gold development opportunities, and integrated mining support services.

Our capabilities include extraction, grade control, sampling, laboratory verification through certified facilities, beneficiation coordination, stockpile management, and coordinated logistics for local and export markets. We also provide mining-related construction services including earthworks, site preparation, infrastructure development, and rehabilitation.

Precision & Quality Control

Our operations are driven by strict attention to detail, from extraction through to final delivery. Consistent grade control and verified laboratory testing ensure reliable, specification-driven output.

Reliability & Consistency

We are committed to dependable performance across supply, logistics, and execution. Our clients rely on us for consistent quality and on-time delivery in demanding market conditions.
